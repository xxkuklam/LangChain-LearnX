# Rapid-Drying Tunnel Paint "TunnelPaint Pro"

**Product Code:** ACME-PNT-TUNEL  
**Category:** Chemical Construction & Illusions  
**List Price:** $89.99 / 500ml can  

### Product Overview
A spray paint that creates a visual and physical illusion of a tunnel on solid rock or concrete walls. Operates selectively: it allows individuals carrying an active ACME SmartID badge to pass through, while remaining a solid wall to unauthorized entities.

### Technical Specifications
* **Coverage:** 1 can = 1 tunnel (3-meter diameter, 50-meter visual depth).
* **Drying Time:** 3.0 seconds post-application.
* **Weather Resistance:** 48 hours (spontaneously evaporates into neutral gas thereafter).

### Guidelines & Liability
* Before running into a painted tunnel, ensure the SmartID wristband indicator light is green.
* ACME Corp assumes no liability for bruises, concussions, or broken bones resulting from attempts to enter a tunnel without a valid user license.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **What happens if a train attempts to enter a painted tunnel?**  
  ACME maintenance trains pass through unobstructed. Third-party vehicles will collide with the wall.
