# Dehydrated Desert Boulder "Instant Boulder"

**Product Code:** ACME-BLD-DRY  
**Category:** Defense & Traps  
**List Price:** $249.00  

### Product Overview
An innovative product allowing easy pocket-sized transportation of a 5-ton granite boulder. Sold as a small pouch containing 100 grams of gray powder.

### Technical Specifications
* **Pre-Hydration Mass:** 100 g.
* **Post-Hydration Mass:** 5,000 kg (5 metric tons).
* **Reaction Catalyst:** 1 drop of any water-containing liquid (water, tea, juice, sweat).
* **Rehydration Time:** 0.05 seconds (explosive expansion).
* **Boulder Shape:** Irregular spheroid with jagged edges.

### Safety Precautions
* **CRITICAL SAFETY:** The reaction is instantaneous! After adding liquid, immediately throw the pouch at least 10 meters away.
* Store in an airtight container with desiccant packets.
* Do not ingest. In case of accidental ingestion followed by water consumption, contact emergency services immediately.
