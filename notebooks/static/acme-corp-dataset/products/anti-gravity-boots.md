### Product Overview
Specialized athletic footwear equipped with negative gravity vector soles. Designed for rapid movement across walls and ceilings, as well as executing vertical jumps up to 15 meters. Features a built-in dynamic ankle stabilization system.

### Technical Specifications
* **Maximum Operational Ceiling:** 15 meters above ground level (safety cutoff engages beyond this height).
* **Maximum User Weight:** 110 kg.
* **Braking System:** Pneumatic impact cushions embedded in the heels.
* **Charging Time:** 30 minutes (standard outlet or USB-C SuperCharge).

### Warranty Conditions & Risks
* Warranty does not cover loss of footwear resulting from accidental atmospheric launch caused by ignoring altitude warnings.
* Do not use near resonant neodymium magnets or heavy concentrations of anvils.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
* **How do I walk up a brick wall in these?**  
  Turn the side dial to "WallRun" mode and maintain an initial approach speed of at least 12 km/h.
